🎯 Market Sniper Engine v1.0.0 Now Live!
Tired of manually checking every data center world just to hunt down the best market board deals? Market Sniper automates the entire process in real-time, matching regional prices directly against your home world as you browse!

📥 How to Install:
Open your Dalamud settings in-game using /xlplugins.
Go to the plugin installer and search for: Market Sniper
Click install, and you are ready to roll!

✨ What It Does:
Instant Background Scans: The second you open any item's listing profile on a Market Board, the engine triggers a background check against the Universalis API.
Unified UI Dashboard: Type /msniper to open a persistent history log that tracks every scanned item, stores local cache, features live item text filtering, and includes right-click clipboard shortcuts.
Smart Margin Thresholds: Features a built-in UI slider so you can customize exactly what percentage of savings triggers a bargain notification.

🎨 Chat Log Color Guide:
The plugin instantly prints price checks to your system chat log using zero-aliasing, color-stabilized UI indicators:

🟢 Green: Your home world is already the cheapest option in the entire region. No travel needed!
🟡 Yellow: A better deal is waiting on another world inside your own Data Center. (Quick world-visit!)
🔴 Red: A massive [MEGA DEAL] is active on a completely different Data Center. (Cross-DC travel required!)

---

🚀 How To Use It:
Open the Dashboard: Type /msniper in your chat bar to open or hide the master tracking window.
Set Your Target Margin: Use the slider at the top of the window to pick your alert threshold (e.g., 30% savings).
Go Shopping: Simply click on any item on a market board to view its prices. The plugin will immediately analyze the entire region and spit out the exact cheapest location and your total Gil savings right into your chat box.
Manage History: In the /msniper menu, you can click [R] to instantly re-scan an old item's prices across the region, or left-click its name to toggle active tracking.

---

⚠️ IMPORTANT: RATE LIMIT WARNING (Read Before Using)
Do NOT Spam Items: Clicking through hundreds of market board items rapidly or constantly hammering the UI refresh buttons is completely useless. Universalis aggregates data and does not update instantly on every click.
IP Block Risk: Aggressive spamming triggers Cloudflare and Universalis API DDoS protection. If you make too many requests in a short window, Universalis will temporarily or permanently block your IP address, which will break your plugin access (and website access) entirely.
Use at Your Own Risk: Browse naturally and give the engine a brief moment between items. The plugin features built-in safety debouncing, but responsible browsing behavior is ultimately up to you!
